Research

Current questions and selected work

My work has moved from optimisation and regularised statistical learning toward probability, stochastic interacting systems, and their continuum limits.

01

Master's thesis · in preparation

Hydrodynamic limits for interacting particle systems

I study how large stochastic particle systems give rise, after rescaling, to deterministic evolution equations. The project brings together Markov processes, scaling limits, local equilibrium, and partial differential equations.

Drone routing for event filming

A mixed-integer optimisation model for battery-constrained drone trips, event time windows, and maximisation of recorded service time.

An audio-classification study using spectral and time-domain features, dimensionality reduction, linear classifiers, and neural approaches on Medley-solos-DB.
