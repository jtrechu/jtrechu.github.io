About

Jaime Díaz-Trechuelo

I am an MSc student in Mathematics at the University of Bonn, interested in probability, stochastic processes, interacting particle systems, and hydrodynamic limits.

Before Bonn, I completed a double BSc in Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Seville and spent an exchange year at the University of Warwick. I am currently preparing my master's thesis on hydrodynamic limits for interacting particle systems.
