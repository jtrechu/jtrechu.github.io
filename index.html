About.

I am a mathematician and statistician from Seville, currently completing an MSc in Mathematics at the University of Bonn. My interests are varied, laying mostly in the applied part of mathematics, and go from probability theory or numerical analysis to statistical learning and optimization. Now, I am focussing on probabilistic interacting particle systems. I am particularly interested in how deterministic macroscopic behaviour emerges from systems built from many random local interactions.

Before Bonn, I completed a double BSc in Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Seville and spent a year abroad at the University of Warwick.
