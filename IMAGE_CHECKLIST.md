# Image checklist

The site works immediately with built-in SVG placeholders. To use real photographs, add the following files exactly at the paths shown.

## 1. Main portrait

**Filename:** `assets/images/jaime-profile.webp`

**What it should be:** A professional but natural portrait, ideally from the waist or chest upward. Use a simple background, direct or slightly off-camera eye contact, and neutral academic clothing. A vertical crop is best.

**Recommended dimensions:** 1200 × 1500 px or larger, approximately 4:5 aspect ratio.

**Crop behaviour:** The site uses `object-fit: cover` and favours the upper-centre of the photograph. Leave some space above the head and avoid placing important details at the very bottom.

## 2. “la Caixa” fellowship ceremony

**Filename:** `assets/images/la-caixa-ceremony.webp`

**What it should be:** A horizontal photograph from the 9 July 2026 fellowship ceremony. Prefer a clear image of Jaime receiving the fellowship or standing at the event. Avoid a very wide group photo where Jaime is difficult to identify.

**Recommended dimensions:** 1600 × 1000 px or larger, approximately 16:10 aspect ratio.

**Crop behaviour:** The image is centre-cropped. Keep Jaime near the centre third.

## File format

WebP is recommended for smaller file sizes. JPEG also works after changing the two image paths in `index.html`. Aim for each image to be under 500 KB after export.
