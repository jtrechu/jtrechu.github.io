Background.

I have been studying the Mathematics MSc at the University of Bonn since September 2025. Before moving to Germany, I completed a double BSc in Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Seville. During 2023–24, I spent an exchange year at the University of Warwick, where I took advanced courses in mathematics and statistics and achieved First Class results in every module.

At Bonn, I have worked as a tutor for Introduction to Numerical Analysis and Markov Processes. I also volunteered with the press team at the 9th European Congress of Mathematics in Seville, contributing to daily coverage and interview translation. Both reflect my interest on teaching and sharing mathematics to broader audiences.

Currently, my studies are supported by the “la Caixa” Postgraduate Studies Abroad Fellowship. Other distinctions include the XVI Andalusian Award for Best Academic Trajectory in Statistics, or a Spanish Ministry of Education Collaboration Scholarship.
