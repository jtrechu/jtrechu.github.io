Birth and death.

Particles die independently at a constant rate of 1, while empty sites can be born from occupied neighbours. Change the birth rate and watch survival or extinction of the particles.

Predator and prey.

Prey reproduce independently into empty sites, while predators only reproduce by invading the spaces occupied by prey. Predators eventually die, but prey only die when taken over by predators.

Voter model.

Three opinions compete through a single local rule: a site repeatedly copies one of its nearest neighbours. The more prevalent one opinion is among your neighbours the more likely they are to influence you.
