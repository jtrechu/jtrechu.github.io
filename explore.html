NOV.2026

Consistency of Breiman Random Forests

Graduate Seminar on Mathematics for Data Science, University of Bonn.

This seminar centered on mathematical foundations of current data science techniques. I worked on Consistency of random forests ↗ by Scornet et al. (2015). I went over the main results and proofs of the article. I focussed on explaining minimal conditions on asymptotic incorrelation in order for Random Forests to be consistent in the L² sense. This is true even in the fully grown case, in which single trees famously suffer from overfitting.

JUN.2026

The Rapid Stirring Limit

Graduate Seminar on Stochastic Modelling, University of Bonn.

This seminar centered on a first study of Interacting Particle Systems (IPSs). I was in charge of one of the talks on the rapid stirring limit. I presented how we study this "rapid-stirring limit" for general, translation invariant, finite-range systems. It serves as a first connection between interacting particle systems as the motor of microscopic dynamics yielding a deterministic PDE solution macroscopically.

SEPT.2025

Drone Scheduling for Filming Time Maximization

PI3 Introduction to Research Program, Mathematics Institute of the University of Seville.

I revisted the problem studied on Filming runners with drones is hard↗ by Diaz-Bañez et al. (2025). We focussed on the case with a single drone, and tackled the problem in an exact manner using a MILP formulation. We presented some schedules produced by the program and discussed possible advantages of the approach.
